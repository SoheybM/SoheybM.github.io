FenRIR — French Robotics Cup 2026

Collaborative association project | RIR (Robotique des Ingénieurs de Roanne), Polytech Lyon

Overview

FenRIR is the main autonomous robot developed by the RIR association and its members for the 2026 French Robotics Cup. Designed around the competition's Winter is Coming theme, it was built to handle wooden game elements with a lift and multiple gripping mechanisms, in coordination with smaller Ratatosk robots (PAMIs).

Engineering scope

The team-built system includes mechanical actuation, stepper-driven motion and elevation, suction-based grippers, electronic control and sensor integration. The association's public documentation describes an ESP32-based control system, LiDAR obstacle detection, a 12 V battery system and several auxiliary mechanisms.

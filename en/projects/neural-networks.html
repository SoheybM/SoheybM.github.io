Neural Networks — Learning and Classification Experiments

GPA671 — Introduction to Artificial Intelligence | ÉTS Montréal | Fall 2026

Overview

A developing collection of practical learning experiments with artificial neurons, perceptrons and multilayer perceptrons (MLPs). Exercises include classification, experiments with different architectures and hyperparameters, and visualizations of nonlinear decision boundaries.

Scope and contributions

Work on algorithm implementation, learning behavior, the role of iteration/batches and model evaluation, with Python as the main programming language. This portfolio focuses on demonstrable implementations and experimental insights, not a full publication of graded coursework or teaching materials.

Evidence and next steps

A bilingual LinkedIn post describes MLP experiments and decision-boundary visualization. The original Python sources and experiment outputs still need to be reviewed before publishing technical claims, numerical results or code.

Hestia — Connected Door Sensor

Fourth-year Industrial Systems & Robotics project | Polytech Lyon

Objective

Build and validate a connected prototype that detects a magnetic state near a door and displays it in a home-automation interface. The concept demonstrates a working sensing-to-dashboard chain, not a certified mechanical lock-status detector.

System

  • ESP32-H2 DevKitM-1 reads a reed switch at GPIO3 and drives an onboard status LED.
  • Zigbee sends the ON/OFF state to Home Assistant, hosted on a Raspberry Pi 3 Model B with a Sonoff Zigbee coordinator.
  • A Home Assistant template entity presents a readable interpreted status.
  • A rechargeable LiPo battery, TP/TC4056 charge module and MT3608 boost converter power the prototype.
  • An initial CAD enclosure explores packaging the electronics.

Implementation and testing

The team adapted an ESP-IDF HA_on_off_light Zigbee example, originally modeled as a lamp, to reflect the reed-switch state. The prototype was paired through ZHA, integrated into Home Assistant, and validated for sensor reading, Zigbee updates, LED behavior and battery-powered operation. Early power-supply testing exposed a voltage-drop issue, resolved by replacing the charging module.

Limitations

The reed switch detects magnet proximity, not whether a lock bolt is mechanically engaged. Further work would require better mechanical sensing, an appropriate Zigbee contact-device profile, battery monitoring, power measurements and enclosure refinement.

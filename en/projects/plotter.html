2D Pen Plotter — Electromechanical Project

Polytech Lyon | 2024–2025

Overview

A student team designed and assembled a two-axis pen plotter capable of automatic drawing, combining a mechanical frame, linear guides, belt-driven motion, motors and a pen-lift actuator. The user has confirmed that automatic operation was successfully demonstrated.

My role

Project lead: project coordination, work breakdown and planning using GanttProject. Further design and implementation responsibilities will be recorded only once verified against the team's records or direct recollection.

The available photos show the constructed frame, motion system, printed components, belts, guides and actuators. These authentic project photographs will be used in the public gallery once their final selection and captions are approved.
